# Ziyu Liu

**Ph.D. student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University · Researcher at Shanghai AI Laboratory**

[Homepage](https://liuziyu77.github.io/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=hUrX-LMAAAAJ) · [Email](mailto:2184708350@qq.com) · [Open-Source Collection](https://liuziyu77.github.io/projects/)

I work on **multimodal large language models**, **AI agents**, **reinforcement learning**, and **reward models**. My current research explores how multimodal agents can learn from interaction, use tools, and improve through reliable feedback.

I am advised by [Prof. Dahua Lin](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=GMzzRRUAAAAJ&hl=en) at SJTU and jointly advised by [Yuhang Zang](https://yuhangzang.github.io/) and [Jiaqi Wang](https://myownskyw7.github.io/) at Shanghai AI Laboratory.

## Recent Research

### Agents

- **WildClawBench**: real-world, long-horizon evaluation for AI agents. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10912) · [Code](https://github.com/InternLM/WildClawBench)
- **SEAgent**: self-evolving computer-use agents that learn autonomously from experience. **ICML 2026**. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04700) · [Code](https://github.com/SunzeY/SEAgent)
- **CODA**: a trainable dual-brain planner-executor for scientific computer use. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20096) · [Code](https://github.com/OpenIXCLab/CODA)
- **Visual-ARFT** (**First Author**): agentic reinforcement fine-tuning for visual search and coding. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14246) · [Code](https://github.com/Liuziyu77/Visual-RFT/tree/main/Visual-ARFT)

### RL & Reward Models

- **Visual-ERM** (**First Author**): fine-grained, interpretable reward modeling for visual equivalence. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13224) · [Code](https://github.com/InternLM/Visual-ERM)
- **ARM-Thinker**: multimodal reward modeling with visual reasoning and agentic tool use. **CVPR 2026**. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05111) · [Code](https://github.com/InternLM/ARM-Thinker)
- **SPARK** (**First Author**): synergistic policy and generative reward co-evolution. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22624) · [Code](https://github.com/InternLM/Spark)
- **Visual-RFT** (**First Author**): visual reinforcement fine-tuning with verifiable rewards. **ICCV 2025**. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01785) · [Code](https://github.com/Liuziyu77/Visual-RFT)
- **MIA-DPO** (**First Author**): multi-image preference optimization for large vision-language models. **ICLR 2025**. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.17637) · [Code](https://github.com/Liuziyu77/MIA-DPO)
